The courts

Two panoramic glass padel courts, floodlit for evening play, open every day of the year subject to weather. Suitable for all levels, from absolute beginners to competitive league players.

Facilities

  • Floodlit play into the evening
  • Equipment hire — bats and balls available on site
  • Toilets and changing space in the park pavilion
  • Refreshments from the park café (seasonal)

Who runs it

Eaton Park Padel is operated by National Tennis Association, the racket sports operator behind tennis and padel programmes across thirteen counties.
