About

Eaton Park Padel

A friendly four-court padel venue set inside Norwich's Eaton Park, alongside the existing tennis courts.

Why we're here

Padel is the fastest-growing racket sport in the world, and Norwich has been waiting for proper courts. Eaton Park Padel sits right next door to Norwich Parks Tennis at Eaton Park, sharing the same operator and the same commitment: world-class facilities, easy access, and a community that welcomes everyone from first-timers to league players.

What we offer

  • Four outdoor panoramic glass courts, floodlit and open all year
  • Live online booking via the Playtomic app — single tap, pay online
  • Beginner-friendly Intro to Padel sessions every week
  • Group coaching and 1-to-1 lessons with LTA Accredited padel coaches
  • Social play and mixed-ability nights so you can turn up alone and find a game
  • Regular box leagues through Matchspace — competitive without the pressure
  • Free racket and ball use to get you started — request one on our racket request form at least 24 hours ahead

Who runs it

Eaton Park Padel is operated by National Tennis Association (NTA), a leading racket-sports operator across the UK, behind tennis and padel programmes including Norwich Parks Tennis — just the other side of the path.

The same team. The same standards. A racket sport for everyone.
