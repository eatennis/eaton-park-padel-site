The fastest-growing sport in the world

Padel is a fast-paced racket sport that blends the best of tennis and squash. Played on a smaller court with glass walls, it's easy to pick up in your first session and addictive by your second. Doubles by default, so you turn up with three mates or join a public game and meet new ones.

Common questions

How do I book a court?

All court bookings are made through the Playtomic app. Download it, search Eaton Park Padel, pick a slot. Full walk-through on the How to book page.

I've never played — where do I start?

Book onto an Intro to Padel session — they run weekly and are designed for absolute beginners. See Coaching for upcoming dates.

Can I borrow a racket?

Yes — to get you started, request a free racket and balls on our racket request form at least 24 hours before you play. A self-service hire locker is coming to the courts this summer.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancel through Playtomic more than 24 hours before your game for a full refund.

Do you run box leagues?

Yes — monthly box leagues run through Matchspace. See the Competition page.

Can I book a corporate or private event?

Yes — drop us a line via the Contact page and we'll set it up.
