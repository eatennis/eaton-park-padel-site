Padel box leagues

Friendly, structured padel played at your level. Our box leagues run through the Matchspace app — meet local players, arrange your own matches, and put your coaching into practice.

How to join

Why join the box leagues?

The box leagues are a friendly, competitive way to meet other players at your level across our padel coaching programmes in Norwich. You're placed in a small box of players of similar ability, play each other over the league, and the winners move up while others move down — so you're always matched with people around your standard.

They give you the chance to:

  • Meet and play with local players at your level.
  • Arrange both competitive and friendly matches, in your own time.
  • Support your coaching — putting what you learn in lessons into independent play.

Everything runs through Matchspace, a free phone app. You find your league, arrange matches with your opponents, and submit your scores — all in one place.

How to download the app and join

Four steps to get set up on Matchspace and into a padel box league.

Step 01

Download the Matchspace app

Get the free Matchspace app on your smartphone, then open it.

📱 iPhone — App Store 🤖 Android — Google Play
Step 02

Create your account

Complete your login details to set up your account. When you're asked for a join code, enter the code for our padel leagues:

Join code XHAGC-8511
Step 03

Complete your settings

A few quick profile settings before you play:

  • Ability level — this doesn't limit what you can play, so just give your best guess.
  • Game availability — let people know when you prefer to play.
  • Looking for a hit — puts a green tag on your profile so others know they can reach out for a friendly game. Turn it on or off any time.
Step 04

Find your league and enter

Head to the Feed tab at the bottom of the app and open the padel box leagues.

Select the event you want to join and follow the prompts — you're in, and ready to start arranging matches.

For more detail, the Matchspace Player Guide (PDF) has extra tips to get you started — the app is straightforward, but the guide is handy first time round.

Any questions, get in touch: norwichleagues@nationaltennis.org.uk
