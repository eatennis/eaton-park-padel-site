How box leagues work

  1. Sign up through Matchspace by the deadline (usually the 1st of each month).
  2. You're placed in a box of six players at your level — based on your previous results and self-rating.
  3. You arrange your five fixtures within the month, directly with your opponents through the app.
  4. Submit your scores. Top two players move up a box next month, bottom two move down.

You book the court yourself through Playtomic for each fixture — Matchspace handles the league, Playtomic handles the courts.

First time entering a box?

You'll be self-rated to start. Boxes are friendly — most people just want regular fixtures with people at their level.
