Opening hours

Floodlights come on when it gets dark.

Parking

Two pay & display car parks on South Park Avenue (NR4 7AU). Contactless only — £2 for up to 4 hours; charges apply 09:00–21:00. Blue Badge parking is free. Full tariffs & season tickets →

By bus

First Norwich service 25 stops on Earlham Road, a 5-minute walk.

By bike

National Cycle Route 1 runs along Earlham Road. Racks on site.

Accessibility

Level access to courts from the park path. Accessible toilet in the pavilion.
