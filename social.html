Players' WhatsApp group

How the group works

It's a simple WhatsApp group for Eaton Park players who want to find others to play with. One friendly group, all abilities — no cost, no commitment, and you can leave whenever you like.

  • Post when you want to play — day, time, and your rough level.
  • See a game that suits you? Reply and sort it out between you.
  • Need a fourth? Drop your Playtomic Open Match link so others can join in one tap.
  • Got your four? Move the details to a private chat and book the court on Playtomic.
  • Keep it to arranging games and be decent to each other.
  • One heads-up: everyone in the group can see your phone number.

How socials work

Pay your spot through Playtomic. Turn up five minutes before the start, meet the host, get put on a court with three others at your level. Sessions are 90 minutes. New to the game? Request a free racket and balls on our racket request form at least 24 hours ahead — an on-site hire locker is coming this summer.

You don't need a partner. You don't need to know anyone. That's the point.

Find a social session

On Playtomic, search Eaton Park Padel and filter by "Public" or "Social". Pick a slot, pay, done. Get the app, or do it all in your browser.
