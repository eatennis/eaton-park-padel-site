Booking

How to book a court

All court bookings are made through the Playtomic app. Here's how — register, book, cancel.

Open Playtomic
Step 01

Download the app

Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android) on your phone and search for "Playtomic".

[Screenshot: images/howto/register-01.jpg]
Step 02

Tap Register

Open the app and tap the blue "Register" button on the welcome screen.

[Screenshot: images/howto/register-02.jpg]
Step 03

Fill in your details

Name, email, and password. Takes about 30 seconds.

[Screenshot: images/howto/register-03.jpg]
Step 04

Add a photo (optional)

Upload a photo if you'd like — it helps people you're playing with recognise you. You're ready to book.

[Screenshot: images/howto/register-04.jpg]
Step 01

Tap "Book a court"

Once you've registered and verified your account, open Playtomic and tap "Book a court" on the home tab.

[Screenshot: images/howto/book-01.jpg]
Step 02

Find the club

Search for "Eaton Park Padel" and tap it.

[Screenshot: images/howto/book-02.jpg]
Step 03

Pick a date and time

Tap "Book", then choose a date and a start time that suits you.

[Screenshot: images/howto/book-03.jpg]
Step 04

Choose your court and duration

Scroll down, pick the court you want and how long for (60 or 90 minutes).

[Screenshot: images/howto/book-04.jpg]
Step 05

Pay and you're done

Choose "Pay your part" to split with the other players, or "Pay everything" to cover the whole court. Confirm and you'll get the booking in your matches list.

[Screenshot: images/howto/book-05.jpg]
Step 01

Open "Play a match"

In Playtomic, tap "Play a match" on the home tab.

[Screenshot: images/howto/cancel-01.jpg]
Step 02

Find your booking

Tap "My matches" top right, then tap the booking you want to cancel.

[Screenshot: images/howto/cancel-02.jpg]
Step 03

Tap the three dots

Top right of the booking screen, tap the three-dot menu.

[Screenshot: images/howto/cancel-03.jpg]
Step 04

"See booking or cancel"

Choose this option from the pop-up.

[Screenshot: images/howto/cancel-04.jpg]
Step 05

Confirm the cancellation

Tap "Cancel booking" at the bottom. Cancellations more than 24 hours before your slot get a full refund.

[Screenshot: images/howto/cancel-05.jpg]

Drop the Playtomic screenshots into 02_Site/images/howto/ using the filenames shown next to each step (e.g. register-01.jpg). Once they're in, the grey boxes will fall away and the photos will show.
