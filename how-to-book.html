Booking

How to book a court

All court bookings are made through the Playtomic app. Here's how — register, book, cancel.

Open Playtomic
Step 01

Download the app

Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android) on your phone and search for "Playtomic".

Step 02

Tap Register

Open the app and tap the blue "Register" button on the welcome screen.

Step 03

Fill in your details

Your name, email, mobile number and a password, then accept the terms and conditions. Marketing emails are optional — leave that box unticked if you'd rather not. Tap "Create an account". Takes about 30 seconds.

Step 04

Add a photo (optional)

Upload a photo if you'd like — it helps people you're playing with recognise you. You're ready to book.

Step 01

Tap "Book a court"

Once you've registered and verified your account, open Playtomic and tap "Book a court" on the home tab.

Step 02

Find the club

Search for "Eaton Park Padel" and tap it.

Step 03

Pick a date and time

Tap "Book", then choose a date and a start time that suits you.

Step 04

Select your court and pay

Scroll down and select the court you want to book and the preferred duration for your reservation.

Choose your payment method by tapping "Pay your part", then complete your booking by selecting "Continue to payment".

Add your crew to the booking, or share them a link.

Step 01

Open Playtomic

Open the Playtomic app on your phone to the home screen.

Step 02

Find your booking

Go to "Your matches" and tap the booking you want to cancel.

Step 03

Open the menu

On the booking screen, tap the three-dot menu in the top right.

Step 04

See booking or cancel

In the Options pop-up, tap "See booking or cancel".

Step 05

Cancel the booking

Scroll down and tap "Cancel booking". Cancel more than 24 hours before your slot for a full refund (2–10 days back to your card, depending on your bank). Inside 24 hours the booking is non-cancellable.
